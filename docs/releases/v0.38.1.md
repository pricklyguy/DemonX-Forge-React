# DemonX Forge 0.38.1

The first update installed through the new updater (Update now, from 0.38.0), plus fixes and a new move gizmo.

- **A bottom information bar again.** What is selected is measured in the bar along the bottom: click a face for its kind and area, an edge for its length (and diameter for a round one), a line in a sketch for its length and angle, a circle for its diameter. **Shift or Ctrl+click** a second face or edge and the bar gives the distance and angle between the two (two parallel faces: the distance between the planes).
- **The Front plane opens the right way up.** Choosing XZ / Front to start a sketch used to look at the plane from behind and upside down. It now opens seen from the front, with X to the right and Z up, like the Top and Side planes. (New sketches only; sketches already made keep their orientation.)
- **A new move and turn gizmo,** for Move / Copy of a body (M in the model view) and of sketch curves (M in a sketch): white arrows along the axes (a dot in a circle when an axis points at you), a small square between two arrows that moves in that plane, a centre dot that moves freely (in the sketch's plane, for a sketch), and a ring with four handles that turns about its axis. It keeps one size on the screen at any zoom, and the numbers in the box follow what you drag. Hold Shift for fine steps; turns snap to 15 degrees (1 with Shift). The old three-arrow handles for Move are replaced by it.
- Updater, workflow time limits and housekeeping from 0.38.0 carry on.

**Tested:** real browser (`e2e/infobar.mjs`, `e2e/triad.mjs`, `e2e/sketchmove.mjs`, `e2e/usability.mjs` and the rest of the suite) against a SolidCraft 0.29.0 engine on Linux; unit tests for the gizmo's maths. **Not tested:** Windows, an actual update from 0.38.0 through Update now, a turn of a body with the rings on a real part, and the gizmo with a touch screen. Whether the gizmo matches the one you have in mind is for you to say: tell me what to change (size, colours, which handles to show).
