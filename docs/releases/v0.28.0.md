# DemonX Forge 0.28.0

Box, Cylinder and Sphere.

- **Three new CREATE buttons: Box, Cylinder, Sphere** (also in the CREATE ▾ menu). Each opens a small dialog with live preview, like Coil: type the size, type where it goes, choose New Body, Join or Cut, OK.
- **Box:** Length (X), Width (Y), Height (Z); the position is its corner or its centre (your choice).
- **Cylinder:** Diameter, Height, the centre of its base and its axis (X, Y, Z or the negative of each).
- **Sphere:** Diameter and the centre.
- **Click a face to place it.** With a dialog open, a click on a face fills in the position from where you clicked; for a Cylinder on a flat face it also turns the axis to stand the cylinder on that face. Everything stays typeable, and any number can be a parameter name.
- **Name the body.** With New Body there is a Body name box; the body is called that in the browser.
- In an empty design the first shape is a new body. After that the default is Join.
- In a medium-width window the three buttons step aside into the CREATE ▾ menu before the others do.

**Tested:** real engine and real browser (`e2e/primitives.mjs`: a named 30 x 20 x 10 box with its corner at the origin; a cylinder stood on its top face by clicking it and joined (15 mm high); a sphere as a new body named Ball, 8 mm across; a zero size is refused; Esc cancels; Ctrl+Z takes one away), and the whole e2e suite. **Not tested:** a body made this way then edited from the timeline (the dialogs make new steps only), Windows.
