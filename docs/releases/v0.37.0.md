# DemonX Forge 0.37.0

Moving and turning things in a sketch.

- **M moves the selected sketch curves.** In a sketch, select curves (click, Shift for more, or drag a box round them) and press **M**, or use CREATE... MODIFY > Move / Copy. A small box opens with **X**, **Y** and **Turn** (degrees) and a **Copy** tick; Enter or OK does it, Esc cancels. In the model view M still moves the chosen body: Forge looks at which one you are in.
- **See it before you do it.** The curves are drawn where they will land while the box is open, and two arrows (red X, green Y) stand at the middle of the selection. Drag an arrow and the X or Y number follows; or type the numbers. Nothing changes in the sketch until OK, and one Ctrl+Z takes it back.
- **A turn lets go of Horizontal and Vertical.** A rectangle's sides carry Horizontal and Vertical constraints, which hold them in place, so turning a rectangle 30 degrees collapsed it to a point. Now a turn first removes those two kinds from the chosen lines, then turns (a turn of 0 or 180 degrees keeps them). Forge says so nowhere else, so if you turn a rectangle and want its sides level again, use the Horizontal / Vertical constraint.
- **A shape sitting on the origin will not move.** A rectangle drawn from the very origin corner is pinned to it by the engine; moving it moves everything but that corner. Draw it a little away from the origin, or delete the Coincident constraint first. (Found while testing; the engine does this, not Forge.)
- The old one-line "dx, dy, angle" box is gone; the new box replaces it.

**Tested:** real browser (`e2e/sketchmove.mjs`: M with and without a selection, typing, dragging the X arrow, OK, undo, Esc, Copy, a turn, a turn plus a move; `e2e/sketchmodify.mjs` updated for the new box), unit tests, the sketch, select, drawing and polish scripts. **Not tested:** Windows, rotating a handle by dragging (the Turn is typed only for now), the Y arrow's drag (the X arrow's is tested; they share the code).
