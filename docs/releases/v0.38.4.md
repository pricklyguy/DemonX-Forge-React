# DemonX Forge 0.38.4

Arrows round the view cube.

- **Four arrows** (up, down, left, right) on the sides of the cube turn the view a quarter turn each, smoothly; the model turns the way the arrow points (the right arrow brings the left face round to the front). They work from any view, including past the top and bottom.
- **Two curved arrows** at the corners roll the view a quarter turn clockwise or anticlockwise about the line of sight.
- The house still goes to the home view, and a click on a face still looks at that side. A click on an arrow while the view is already turning is ignored, so quick clicks never jump.

**Tested:** real browser (`e2e/viewcube.mjs`: each arrow and roll, the view direction and up direction after each, and a double click), the view tests in the rest of the suite. **Not tested:** Windows, and the arrows with a touch screen.
