# DemonX Forge 0.38.3

Less lag when moving bodies, drag-select in the model view, and the sketch Move box in the same style as the body one.

- **Move no longer lags.** Dragging a gizmo arrow, handle or square, or typing a number in Move / Copy, used to ask the engine for a new preview after every change (undo, move, rebuild the whole model), which made a detailed part crawl. The preview is now drawn in the view under a matrix, with no engine call at all; the engine does the move once, when you press OK. A copy shows the original in place and the moved copy beside it. Press Pull, Fillet and the other tools still preview through the engine as before.
- **Drag to select bodies.** In the model view, press the left button on empty space and drag: left to right takes the bodies wholly inside the box (window), right to left takes any body the box touches (crossing). Shift or Ctrl adds to the selection. In a body tool (Move, Mirror, patterns and so on) the box adds bodies to the ones being worked on. A drag that takes nothing leaves the selection as it was.
- **Move starts on the bodies you selected.** Bodies chosen whole (in the browser, by double-click or by a drag box) are the ones M picks up; before, M ignored them and asked for a click.
- **Sketch Move / Copy looks like the body one.** The same panel on the left (Curves selected, Step, X, Y and Turn with minus and plus buttons, Copy, OK), instead of the strip along the bottom. The gizmo is unchanged.
- The "move a sketch's plane" turn is not in yet (see the README's list); the sketch gizmo carries on until then.

**Tested:** real browser (`e2e/bodybox.mjs`: window and crossing drags, Shift adds, a drag over nothing keeps the selection, Move leaves the engine alone until OK; `e2e/tools3.mjs`, `e2e/usability.mjs`, `e2e/triad.mjs`, `e2e/sketchmove.mjs` and the rest of the suite against a SolidCraft 0.29.0 engine); unit tests for the preview matrix. **Not tested:** Windows, how much faster it feels on your detailed part (the sandbox has no GPU and no such part), and a drag box over a very large mesh.
