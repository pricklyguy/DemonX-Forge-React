# DemonX Forge 0.38.2

The move / turn gizmo, reworked from your notes on 0.38.1.

- **Turn handles are small discs on a short arc,** not full rings. Each handle sits at a fixed place on the object and lies square to its axis: RX turns about X and sits toward +Z, RY about Y toward +X, RZ about Z toward +Y (for a sketch, the one handle turns about the sketch's normal and sits toward the sketch's Y). From a side the discs show edge-on; they come into view as you orbit. The arc is about a quarter of a circle and fades at its ends; the handle and arc light up in the accent colour when the pointer is over them.
- **The gizmo turns with the object.** While you turn a body or a sketch selection, the arrows, squares and handles turn with it, and the preview follows. A drag of a handle is measured on the handle's own plane, so the turn follows the pointer even when the plane is seen at an angle.
- **Body turns are about the middle of the body** (they used to be about the world origin, so a body far from the origin swung away). A turn is still about one world axis at a time.
- **Move leaves a ghost.** While a Move (not a copy) is previewed, the place the body came from is shown as a faint outline; OK removes the original and keeps the moved body. A Copy leaves the original in place and adds the new body, as before.
- Arrows and squares are unchanged.

**Tested:** real browser (`e2e/triad.mjs`: arrows, square, centre dot and handle in a sketch and on a body, the gizmo turning with the body, a quarter turn about the body's middle), the Move tests in the rest of the suite; unit tests. **Not tested:** Windows, hover colours by eye, handles with touch.
